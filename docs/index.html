How it works

Four steps, one transaction

01Point it at your mint

Paste your token address, pick the round length and your cut. No approval, no waiting, no gatekeeper.

02Send your fees in

Creator fees become the pot instead of sitting idle. Anyone can top it up — that instruction is permissionless.

03Holders play

Buy the last slot by burning your token. Every buy resets the clock and shortens it. Last one holding takes the pot.

04Supply falls

Every buy destroys tokens against your mint. Not moved to a wallet — burned. Your supply only goes one way.

Guarantees

Impossible, not promised

Every claim below is a property of the deployed program, not a policy. All four are checkable in the source in about ten minutes.

✓We cannot touch your pot

The platform account isn't even in the settle instruction. There is no code path by which we take, block or redirect a payout. It isn't that we won't — there's nothing to call.

✓Fees are capped in code

Platform share can never exceed 10%, registration never above 5 SOL, your own cut never above 20%. The program rejects anything higher at registration.

✓Your settings freeze on attach

Timer, price floor, step, both cuts — fixed the moment you register. Nobody can tilt a running game, including you.

✓Payout needs nobody's permission

settle is permissionless and always pays the last buyer, never the caller. If our keeper dies, any player can settle and the winner is still paid.

Game modes

One is built. The rest are specced.

builtLast Man

Buy the last slot. Nobody buys after you before the clock ends, you take the pot. Each buy shortens the clock by 2s, floor at 30s.

speccedThe Siege

One wallet holds a castle with hit points. Attackers burn to damage it, the holder burns to repair. Only the biggest attacker takes it — so attackers race each other while fighting the holder.

speccedThe Heist

Co-op. Burn a target collectively before the deadline and everyone who contributed splits the pot. Miss it and the target comes down. The only mode where players want more players.

speccedThe Split

Two sides, burn to back one, bigger side takes the pot. Weighted by wallet count rather than amount, so a whale can tip the result but cannot take the prize.

Where this actually is

The program is written and 21 tests pass on a local validator — including that two games cannot reach into each other's pots, that you cannot play one game with another token, and that the platform authority has no route to any pot.

It is not audited and not currently deployed. It will hold money for every token that attaches, so it needs a proper audit before it holds anyone's. Stated here rather than buried.
