The numbers

  1. Buy the last slot by burning tokens.
  2. Every buy resets the clock — and shortens it.
  3. Each buy costs more than the last. Resets every round.
  4. Last buyer when the clock hits zero takes the whole pot.

Fees: of the trading fees that come in, 10% funds the project and 90% goes to the pot. That split is taken on the way in, fixed in the program, and visible on every deposit. Nothing is taken from a payout — winners get the whole pot.

The pot sits in a program account with no private key in existence — not the developer's, not anyone's. Payout is a rule in the deployed code: once the clock is up anyone can trigger it, and it always pays the last buyer, never whoever pressed the button. If our keeper stops, any player can settle and the winner is still paid. Tokens spent are destroyed against the mint. Every claim here is checkable on-chain in ten seconds.
